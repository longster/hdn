Greater Houston, Texas

Where Houston's Deaf community connects

Houston Deaf Network is a community project run solo by Long Duong , a Houston local. Not a nonprofit, no office, no direct services for individuals, just one place to find local resources, events, and each other. Businesses can support the community through featured listings and partnerships.

is live

HoustonDeafNetwork isn't going anywhere. It's now part of Echode, which runs the same directory, events, and jobs for Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth, San Antonio, and statewide Texas. If you know someone in those cities, send them there.

About us

Houston Deaf Network is a community run hub for Deaf and Hard of Hearing neighbors across Greater Houston. We gather local resources, events, and opportunities in one place so nobody has to hunt for them. Connection builds the network, sharing keeps it alive.

Questions

Is Houston Deaf Network a nonprofit?

No. It is a community driven project run by a neighbor, not an organization with staff or services.

How do I list my business or event?

Send the details through the contact form below. Listings are free and reviewed before they go live.

Do you help with interpreter placement?

We point you to local agencies instead of placing interpreters directly.
